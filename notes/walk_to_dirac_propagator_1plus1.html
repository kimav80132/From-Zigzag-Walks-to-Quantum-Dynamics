What is known, and what is different here.

That the telegraph process gives the Dirac equation only with an imaginary rate is the result of Gaveau, Jacobson, Kac and Schulman. That a walk which also runs backward in time avoids the imaginary rate is the thesis of McKeon and Ord. That the Dirac propagator in one space dimension is a sum over zigzags is Feynman’s.

What is different here is the order of the steps. The clock of the walk is , and its two counters are ordinary probabilities. The sign that makes the quarter-turn is not put into the construction. It appears at step 6, when the same two equations are read with as the clock.

What is not claimed. This is one space dimension. It rederives known equations and does not predict a new number. The column here can stay real, which describes a neutral particle; a charge needs a second pair of counters. Measurement is not discussed.
